Hi, I'm Aahil 👋

MSc researcher at the University of Ottawa (MIRL Lab), affiliated with the Vector Institute. I work on reinforcement learning for robots that keep learning — agents that adapt over a lifetime instead of being trained once and frozen.

I came to RL from finance and logistics, which shaped how I think: real systems are non-stationary, resets are expensive, and the assumptions baked into a benchmark matter as much as the algorithm.

🔬 Research interests
Continual RL — loss of plasticity, catastrophic forgetting, and the stability–plasticity trade-off in agents that never stop learning
Reset-free / autonomous RL — learning without oracle resets or human-in-the-loop episode boundaries
Robot learning — manipulation, sim-to-real transfer, domain randomization, and massively parallel simulation
World models — latent dynamics models and model-based RL (Dreamer-style architectures)
